06 FAQ

Questions?
We've got answers.

Everything you need to know before starting your next website project.

Can I customize a ready-made website?+

Yes. Choose a website you like, then tell the developer what you want changed. You can customize colors, pages, content, features and more.

How do I communicate with the developer?+

Every project gets a dedicated workspace where you can chat, share files, review designs and keep all project decisions in one place.

How does protected payment work?+

For custom projects, payments can be split into milestones. You approve each milestone before the next payment is released.

What if I don't know which website I need?+

Use “Tell us what you need” and describe your business, budget and goals. We'll help you narrow down the right option.
