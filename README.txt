# Chhath Geet Website

A mobile-first Chhath Puja devotional music landing page inspired by the visual style of the supplied reference.

## Add your songs
Put your own audio files in `assets/` and name them:
- song1.mp3
- song2.mp3
- song3.mp3

Then open `index.html`.

The browser may require a user click before audio can play; the Play button handles that.
